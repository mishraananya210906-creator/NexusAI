
NEXUSAI ACCOUNT SUPPORT GUIDE

LOGIN ISSUES:
Users can log in using their registered email address and password.
If a user forgets their password, they can select the Forgot Password option.
A password reset link will be sent to their registered email.

ACCOUNT UPDATES:
Users can update their registered email address through Account Settings.
Email changes require verification.

ACCOUNT DELETION:
Users can request account deletion through Account Settings.
The deletion request must be verified before processing.

SECURITY:
Users should never share their passwords or verification codes.
For suspicious account activity, contact the support team.
