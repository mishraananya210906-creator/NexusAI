NEXUSAI BILLING SUPPORT GUIDE

PAYMENT ISSUES:
Users can check their payment history in Billing Settings.
If a payment fails, users should verify their payment method.

DUPLICATE PAYMENTS:
If a user is charged twice for the same transaction,
they should contact the billing support team.
The support team will investigate duplicate charges.

REFUNDS:
Refund requests can be submitted through Billing Settings.
Refund eligibility depends on the applicable refund policy.

SUBSCRIPTION BILLING:
Users can view their subscription charges and billing dates
in the Subscription section.
